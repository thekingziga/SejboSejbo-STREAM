# SEJBOSEJBO.TV (Sejbosejbo Live) 🎭🔴

> The official chaotic live streaming platform for the Sejbosejbo universe — blending **Twitch**, **YouTube Live**, and **Owncast** in pure Neo-Brutalist `sejbosejbo.fyi` / `sejbosejbo-app` style.

## ✨ Features

- 🔴 **Live Stream Stage & Channel Ribbon**: Switch seamlessly between live channels (`SEJBOSEJBO OFFICIAL`, `THE SWEAT`, `DR. MIKROVALOVKA`, `KLET LO-FI & MEME RADIO`, or spin up your own channel at `/browse`).
- ⚡ **Live Stream Sejbometer™**: Viewers vote `▲ SEJ BO` or `▼ SEJ NE BO` in real time. Hitting **95%+ MAXIMUM SEJBOSEJBO** triggers a synchronized siren, screen shake, and emoji rain across all connected browsers.
- 💬 **Real-Time WebSocket Chaos Chat**:
  - Custom Sejbosejbo emotes (`:sejbo:`, `:nebo:`, `:cap:`, `:sweat:`, `:brain:`, `:microwave:`, `:cat:`, `:fire:`).
  - Live Streamer Pinned Banner & Interactive **Live Predictions / Polls**.
  - **Bottle Cap (`🧢`) Wallet & Chaos Rewards**: Viewers automatically earn `+5 🧢` every 15 seconds watching and can spend them to trigger live on-stream alerts (*Microwave Ice Alert*, *Brain.exe Crash*, *Maximum Sejbo Siren*, *Bow to the Bottle Cap*, *Emoji Storm*).
- 🎥 **Browser Go-Live Studio (`/studio`) + OBS / Owncast RTMP & HLS**:
  - Broadcast directly from your browser using **Webcam + Mic**, **Screen Share**, or the **60fps Procedural Chaos Synthesizer** with custom lower-third banners—no OBS required!
  - Or connect **OBS Studio / Owncast / MediaMTX** via RTMP & `.m3u8` HLS playback (`hls.js` built-in).
- ✂️ **Instant Clips & VOD Archive (`/clips`)**:
  - Click **✂️ CLIP THAT SEJBO** on any live stream to capture a snapshot clip immediately, or upload MP4/WebM recordings with community voting (`▲ Sej bo` / `▼ Sej ne bo`) and comment threads.
- 🎛️ **Multi-Sejbo 2x2 Quad Monitor Wall (`/multistream`)**: Watch 4 chaotic streams simultaneously.
- 🧢 **The Bottle Cap 24/7 Telemetry Shrine (`/sweat`)**: Dedicated live security cam for the 1 (one) Bottle Cap with persisted worship counter.
- 🌓 **Zero-Flash Light & Dark Mode + ENG / SLO Bilingual Support + Official Theme Anthem (`🔊`)**.
